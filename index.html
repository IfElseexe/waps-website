Three services that keep your pumps working.

Our customer support team looks at the whole process challenge before recommending a pump package, then supports it for its working life.

Installation and commissioning

We set your pump up correctly on site so it runs to specification and as energy-efficiently as possible, which lowers long-term operating costs.

  • Onsite installation
  • Commissioning to your specification
  • Performance and energy optimisation

On-site training

Standard or customised courses so your engineers, operators and maintenance staff know how to apply, specify, install and run pumps efficiently.

  • Tailored to your applications
  • Reduces power consumption
  • Improves pump reliability

Repairs and troubleshooting

Our technicians find the root cause of a pump failure, then repair it on your site or at our own facility with as little downtime as possible.

  • Failure analysis
  • Planned and unplanned repairs
  • Onsite or at our facility

Industries we work in.

The same pump principles apply from a mine pit to a food plant. These are the duties our range is chosen for.

Oil and gas

Crude oil transfer and export, water injection, well servicing, riser boost, drilling mud feed and sludge management.

Mining and minerals processing

Pit and mine dewatering, slurry transfer and sump evacuation.

Water and waste water

Municipal water supply, irrigation, reverse osmosis feed and waste water sludge.

Manufacturing and processing

Chemical, paint, acid and alkaline transfer, plus recirculation and process water.

Food and beverage

Chocolate, yoghurt, syrup and concentrated fruit juice transfer.

Power generation

Boiler feed and cooling circuits, backed by on-site training for operators and maintenance teams.
