Pump users deserve honest technical advice.

Our story, our quality commitments, and the team behind WAPS.

Two entrepreneurs set out to change the way pump users in West Africa deal with their suppliers.

The goal was a company that gives pump users honest, technical advice and high quality equipment to support their operations.

Focusing on that goal has grown WAPS into a recognised and trusted pumping solutions provider across West Africa, with an ever-increasing fleet of pumps in the marketplace and many satisfied customers.

Years on, the pioneering spirit that inspired the founders still drives us. We aim for first class customer support while we keep growing and developing.

What we do

West African Pumping Services Ltd serves the oil and gas industry with:

  • Procurement, supply and maintenance of heavy duty equipment
  • Crude oil export pumps
  • Static and rotating equipment
  • Installation and commissioning of pumps and engines
  • Sludge management and crude oil recovery
