Slurry transfer

Project
Pumping slurry out of several collection sumps.
Solution
2-inch and 3-inch diaphragm pumps with Teflon elastomers.

Challenges

  • The pump had to be portable.
  • The slurry's chemical content and density were demanding.
  • Pumping had to work from negative suction at different sump depths.
  • Different sump levels called for different flow ranges.

Result

The pumps met the required performance, and the Teflon internals handled the slurry's density and chemistry. Wilden air-operated diaphragm pumps suited the job because of their high suction lift and dry-run capability. They need no electricity, and carry handles made them easy to move between sumps.

Dewatering overhaul

Request
Replace a competitor's pump and fit a suitable wet end to an existing CAT C13 engine.
Solution
A Pioneer PP86C17 wet end.

Challenges

  • Deliver the highest flow and head the C13 engine could provide.
  • Be reliable, efficient and consistent in performance.

Result

The PP86C17 was chosen to perform to the end of the pump curve on the CAT C13. WAPS installed it, and it has performed as expected since commissioning. The Pioneer range's newer technology and modern design are credited for its record in dewatering work.

Mine dewatering

Project
Pump water at 450 m³/hr against a 150-metre head.
Solution
Pioneer 150CX dewatering pump.

Challenges

  • A self-priming pumpset for mining pits.
  • A reliable, efficient pump that holds the required performance.
  • Local availability of spare parts and call-out service.

Result

The 150CX was chosen as a proven performer in high-head, high-flow dewatering. Its integrated vacuum pump primes quickly, which reduces fuel consumption and overall cost of ownership. WAPS also provided free onsite pump training and Pump Health Checks.

Strip solution

Project
A pump rated at 64 m³/hr and 9 bar for 120 °C strip solution.
Solution
Netzsch progressive cavity pump, model NSANM105SY02S12V.

Challenges

  • Previous pumps were not achieving the required pressure or flow.
  • Pressure, viscosity or temperature could change.
  • High temperatures called for special care over pump materials.

Result

A reduced-wall-thickness stator and an undercut rotor suit high-temperature service. Progressive cavity pumps tolerate fluctuations in the system, which makes them a good fit for strip solution. The pump was selected so that flow and pressure matched the customer's current requirements.
