Blackmer

Sliding vane pump maker with a history back to 1903. Built in the USA and known for reliable operation and low maintenance cost, these pumps have long been used for petroleum products and liquefied gases.

Ask about Blackmer pumps

Sliding vane pumps

Vanes sit in slots in the rotor and move outward until they press against the casing bore. This forms pumping chambers that draw fluid in at the suction port and push it out of the discharge port. Each turn of the rotor moves a fixed volume of fluid.

Flow
Up to 505 m³/hr
Pressure
24 bar maximum
Materials
Cast iron, ductile iron, 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Chocolate transfer, diesel unloading, caustic soda transfer

Jessberger

Makes electric and pneumatic drum pumps, vertical and horizontal eccentric screw pumps and plastic centrifugal pumps at its German plant. Also supplies nozzles, discharge hoses, flowmeters, thread adapters and strainers.

Ask about Jessberger pumps

Vertical plastic pumps

Resin-encased vertical centrifugal pumps for highly aggressive liquids such as acids and alkalis. A direct-drive electric motor runs at up to 3000 rpm. There is no internal mechanical seal, and the open impeller can pump very dirty liquids continuously.

Flow
6 to 75 m³/hr
Head
7 to 38 m
Column length
500, 800, 1000 or 1250 mm
Materials
PP or PVDF
Typical uses
Chemical transfer, water treatment return, sump evacuation

Horizontal plastic pumps

Resin-encased horizontal centrifugal pumps with a direct-drive motor of up to 3000 rpm, for fast transfer and drainage of aggressive liquids. The open impeller handles dirty liquids up to 500 cps apparent viscosity at 20 °C, with small suspended solids.

Flow
6 to 75 m³/hr
Head
7 to 38 m
Materials
PP or PVDF
Typical uses
Acid transfer, alkaline transfer

Drum pumps

For quick, intermittent transfer of medium-viscosity liquids, neutral or aggressive, from barrels and containers. A long tube connects to an electric or pneumatic motor, in a range of tube materials and lengths.

Flow
Up to 200 l/min
Head
28 m maximum
Tube materials
PP, PVDF, aluminium or 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Diesel and fuel transfer, chemical transfer, alcohol transfer

Mouvex

Eccentric disc pump innovator since 1906, manufactured in France. The pumps handle shear-sensitive liquids with strong suction, and the seal-less design avoids mechanical seals and the leaks they can cause.

Ask about Mouvex pumps

Eccentric disc pumps

A cylinder and a pumping element are mounted on an eccentric shaft. As the shaft turns, the element creates chambers that expand at the intake port to draw fluid in and shrink at the discharge port to push it into the pipework.

Flow
Up to 50 m³/hr
Pressure
10 bar maximum
Materials
Cast iron, bronze, 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Paint transfer, yoghurt, syrup transfer

Netzsch

Progressive cavity and rotary lobe pumps for a wide range of uses. Its German factory builds more than 50,000 pumps a year.

Ask about Netzsch pumps

Progressive cavity pumps

Positive displacement pumps in which a single-helix rotor turns eccentrically inside a double-helix stator, moving the fluid toward the discharge end. Available in many configurations, they can handle almost any medium for intermittent, continuous or dosing duty.

Flow
Up to 500 m³/hr
Pressure
Up to 48 bar (240 bar on special request)
Temperature
-20 to 200 °C
Typical uses
Strip solution, chemical transfer, water re-injection

Rotary lobe pumps

Compact, high-efficiency pumps for viscous liquids that contain solids and abrasives. A pair of abrasion-resistant lobes turns in opposite directions inside a housing to give metered pumping.

Flow
3 to 905 m³/hr
Pressure
Up to 10 bar
Drive
Electric motor, diesel engine or hydraulic motor
Typical uses
Waste water sludge, oilfield drilling mud feed, concentrated fruit juice

NOV

NOV's engineered pump packages have worked in oil and gas for over 40 years. Duplex, triplex and quintuplex reciprocating pumps serve crude oil transfer and high-pressure water injection.

Ask about NOV pumps

Reciprocating pumps

Positive displacement pumps in which a piston or plunger moves back and forth in a cylinder, used where very high pressures are needed. Mechanical efficiency is about 90% and volumetric efficiency about 95%. Capacity depends on piston or plunger area and travel rate.

Plunger pumps
Up to 340 m³/hr, up to 8,000 psi (551 bar), 2 to 1,069 HP
Piston pumps
Up to 159 m³/hr, up to 1,800 psi (124 bar), 4 to 258 HP
Intermittent duty
Up to 340 m³/hr, up to 20,000 psi (1,378 bar), 33 to 2,250 HP
Typical uses
Well servicing, riser boost, dewatering

Pioneer

Centrifugal pumps for mobile or stationary use, built in Europe, South Africa, Australia and America. There are five product families, and the highest-performance dry-prime range delivers flows above 9,900 m³/hr and pressures above 20 bar.

Ask about Pioneer pumps

Centrifugal dewatering pumps

Engine- or electric-driven self-priming pumps for dewatering mines, quarries and construction sites. The robust build and dry-running ability suit harsh West African conditions, and a 50 cfm vacuum pump helps them prime quickly and efficiently.

Flow
Up to 9,900 m³/hr (2,775 l/s)
Head
260 m maximum
Mounting
Skid or trailer
Typical uses
Mine dewatering, dust suppression, well pointing, light slurry handling

SAER

Italian maker of surface centrifugal and submersible pumps, established in 1951. The pumps are designed for clear water applications and built in a fully mechanised factory.

Ask about SAER pumps

Vertical multistage pumps

In-line pumps supplied bareshaft or as a complete pumpset with motor, for high-pressure clear water duty. Pressure builds as the liquid passes through several impellers, or stages.

Flow
Up to 45 m³/hr
Head
Up to 40 bar
Materials
Cast iron, carbon steel, 304 or 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Reverse osmosis systems, gland service, high-pressure systems

Horizontal multistage pumps

For high-pressure, high-flow clear water duty, supplied bareshaft or as a complete pumpset with motor. Like the vertical type, they use several impellers in stages.

Flow
Up to 850 m³/hr
Head
Up to 64 bar
Materials
Cast iron, carbon steel, 304 or 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Boiler feed, municipal water supply, irrigation systems

Borehole pumps

Electro-submersible units with the motor running underwater. The water-filled motor is lubricated and cooled by the water, which keeps the pumped liquid uncontaminated. The motor is close coupled to a multistage pump.

Flow
Up to 725 m³/hr
Head
Up to 95 bar
Materials
Cast iron, bronze, 316 stainless steel
Typical uses
Seawater lift, irrigation systems, municipal water supply

Close or long coupled pumps

Single-stage standard centrifugal pumps in close or long coupled form, driven by a suitably rated electric motor. They are not self-priming.

Flow
Up to 2300 m³/hr (639 l/s)
Head
Up to 100 m
Materials
Cast iron, 316 or 304 stainless steel, carbon steel
Typical uses
Process water, recirculation, irrigation, heating and air-conditioning systems

Tsurumi

A broad range of submersible pumps for mining, wastewater and construction. Founded in Japan in 1924.

Ask about Tsurumi pumps

Submersible dewatering pumps

A hermetically sealed motor is close coupled to the pump body and runs submerged in the liquid being pumped. A mechanical seal system keeps the liquid out of the motor.

Flow
Up to 700 m³/hr
Pressure
160 m maximum
Discharge port
50 to 250 mm
Typical uses
Slurry transfer, sump evacuation, pit dewatering

Wilden

Leading maker of air-operated double diaphragm pumps since Jim Wilden introduced the first one in 1955, refined ever since with feedback from end users.

Ask about Wilden pumps

Air-operated double diaphragm pumps

Compressed-air-driven positive displacement pumps that push fluid out of one of two liquid chambers on each stroke. They suit many transfer jobs, can prime themselves and are intrinsically safe because they have no electrical parts.

Flow
Up to 64 m³/hr
Pressure
Up to 20 bar
Sizes
¼" to 3"
Typical uses
Slurry transfer, chemical transfer, wastewater transfer
