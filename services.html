Support that keeps pumps running.

Installing the right equipment and supporting it well extends equipment life, reduces downtime and lowers operating costs.

Installation and commissioning

Our onsite installation and commissioning service makes sure your pump is set up correctly and operating to your specification.

By installing and commissioning the pump, we can optimise its performance so it runs as energy-efficiently as possible, which keeps long-term operating costs down.

Contact your local team

On-site training

Improve pump reliability by making sure your operators and maintenance teams know how to apply, specify, install and operate your pumping equipment.

Our standard and customisable pump training courses help your pumps run efficiently, with lower power consumption, and can be tailored to your own requirements and applications.

Who should attend? We recommend the standard course to every industry that uses pumps, including water, waste water, mining, minerals processing, oil and gas, power generation, food and beverage, processing and paper manufacturing. Attendees range from engineers to operators and maintenance staff.

Contact your local team

Repairs and troubleshooting

Failure analysis depends on understanding how a combination of symptoms points to the underlying cause. Our service technicians are trained to investigate and find the root cause of any pump failure, and to recommend how to fix it.

Whether on site or at our own facility, we help with unexpected or planned repairs to get your equipment running again with minimum downtime.

Contact your local team

Engineering expertise.

Our customer support team works closely with end users on every process challenge, so the best pump package is chosen for each application. We provide expert engineering consulting in pump design, application and the mechanical systems around them, for the world's leading organisations in mining, oil and gas and manufacturing.

  • Mechanical inspections of existing pumps
  • In-country spares stockholding
  • Troubleshooting existing pumps
  • Review of plans and specifications for new pump installations
  • Project support and application engineering
  • Testing and certification
  • After sales support
  • Replacement pump equipment specifications and analysis
